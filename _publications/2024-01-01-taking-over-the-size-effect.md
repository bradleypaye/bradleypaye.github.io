We show that merger announcement returns account for virtually all of the measured size premium. Small firms earn higher average returns than large firms almost entirely because small firms are more likely to become takeover targets, and takeover announcements generate large positive returns. Asset pricing models that include a factor based on the takeover characteristic outperform otherwise similar models that include the conventional size factor. We conclude that the takeover factor should replace the conventional size factor in benchmark asset pricing models.
