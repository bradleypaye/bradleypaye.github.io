We compare alternative measures of aggregate cash payouts to shareholders as predictors of stock returns. Net payout yield &mdash; which accounts for both dividends and net share repurchases &mdash; produces more accurate return forecasts relative to alternatives, including the traditional dividend yield. In an economic-value exercise, an investor relying on an incorrect payout measure would be willing to pay an economically significant "management fee" to switch to the optimal policy.
