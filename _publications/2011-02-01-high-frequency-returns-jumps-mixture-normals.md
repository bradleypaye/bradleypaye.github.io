Microstructure noise in high-frequency price data can distort the distributional properties of standardized daily stock returns. We show that using a jump-robust volatility estimator (bipower variation) in place of standard realized volatility to standardize returns brings the resulting distribution much closer to normal, consistent with the mixture-of-normals hypothesis. This is evidence that price jumps, rather than microstructure noise, are the more important feature for models of daily stock returns to accommodate.
