Conditional on volatility and other firm characteristics, firms with more negatively skewed performance shocks adopt more conservative corporate policies, including greater cash holdings, a lower likelihood of dividend payments and increases in payout levels, and less financial leverage. These findings suggest that managers respond to crash risk &mdash; not just overall volatility &mdash; when setting corporate policy.
