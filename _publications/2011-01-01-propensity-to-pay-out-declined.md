Prior research documents a decline in the propensity of firms to pay dividends and interprets this as evidence that firms have become less willing to distribute cash to shareholders. We find no evidence that the conditional propensity to distribute net cash to equity holders has declined over the past three decades once repurchases are considered alongside dividends. Conditional on firm characteristics, net payout yields have actually been increasing over time.
