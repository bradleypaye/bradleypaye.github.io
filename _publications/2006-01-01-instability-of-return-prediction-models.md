We test the stability of standard stock-return prediction models &mdash; based on predictors such as the dividend yield, short interest rate, term spread, and default premium &mdash; across a range of international equity indices. We find evidence of parameter instability for most countries in our sample. The United States and United Kingdom both exhibit a structural break around 1974, while a cluster of European countries exhibits breaks between 1978 and 1982.
